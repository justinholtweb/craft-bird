Rate matching

Matched on
the money

Commerce rounds tax to the cent per line. Divide it back out and 21% is not 21% any more.

  • A percentage look-up would refuse ordinary orders
  • An unmapped rate refuses the order and names the gap
Line net€10.10
Tax Commerce recorded€2.12
Derived percentage20.99%
Rate Bird books it under21%  NL high

Bird looks for the mapped rate whose arithmetic lands within a cent — not for a percentage that no shop has ever configured.
